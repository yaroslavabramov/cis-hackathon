THE PROBLEM

Bank security does two things.

It keeps attackers out. It stops customers from being tricked.

And when it catches an attacker, it blocks him.

A block stops one attacker and teaches the bank nothing.
The hole he came for stays open for the next one.

THE IDEA

We don't block him. We let him in.

attacker→La Mirage gateway→ risk ≥ 70 → his own copy of the bank
customers→La Mirage gateway→real bank

The copy runs our real code. Every rule he breaks in there
is a real bug in production.

HOW IT WORKS

Five steps, all automatic

01

Score

Every request through the gateway gets a risk score. Plain rules, no model in the request path.

02

Divert

Above 70 his session moves to a Daytona sandbox from a warm pool in under half a second. Private, no internet, one per attacker.

03

Observe

We check the bank's own rules on everything he does. Each broken rule becomes a finding with the exact request.

04

Fix

When he goes quiet, the findings go to Devin through its API. Devin opens a pull request with the fix and tests.

05

Prove

A fresh sandbox runs the old and the fixed code side by side and replays his exact requests.
